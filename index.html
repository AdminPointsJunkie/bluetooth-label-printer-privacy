Bluetooth Label Printer privacy policy

DrillOps publishes Bluetooth Label Printer, the Android app with the ALL DAY kitchen prep workflow. Effective 20 November 2026.

What stays on your phone

The app stores your prep items, chosen photos, notes, staff initials, usage counts and settings on your device so you can use them again. DrillOps receives no personal data, analytics or advertising identifiers from the app. The app has no account system.

Printing and backups

Nearby devices permission lets you select a paired D450 printer. When you print, the app sends the label to that printer using Bluetooth. Photo and document pickers let you choose individual files. If you export a backup, the app writes the library, including its photos and staff initials, to the destination you choose. Anyone you give a backup to can read it.

Removing data

Delete saved items in the prep library. Clear the app’s storage in Android settings or uninstall it to remove the entire local library. Delete exported copies separately from the location you chose.
