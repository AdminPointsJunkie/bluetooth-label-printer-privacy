Bluetooth Label Printer privacy policy

DrillOps publishes Bluetooth Label Printer for the ALL DAY café kitchen. Effective 20 November 2026.

On your phone

The app saves your prep library locally. Staff shortcuts, default initials, printer selection, label dimensions and print counts also stay on your phone. The app has no advertising or analytics.

Shared café backup

From version 1.2, entering the shared café code in Settings connects your phone to the ALL DAY backup. The app automatically sends existing and newly saved prep items, item names, shelf lives, quantities, notes, custom fields and chosen item photos to DrillOps’ Supabase service over HTTPS. If you include personal details in these fields or photos, those details are included. Saved changes wait on the phone while it is offline. Staff who have the café code can restore the café’s saved items to another phone. The app also downloads the café’s label artwork. You can print one-off labels without saving them to the shared library.

Printing and file exports

Nearby devices permission connects to your selected paired Bluetooth Classic printer. The app holds the connection between prints until it disconnects. The system photo and document pickers let you choose individual files. Export writes a local backup, including any photos and staff initials, to the location you select.

Retention and removal

Deleting a prep item on one phone leaves its backup available for recovery. DrillOps retains backed-up revisions until the café requests removal. To request removal of a café item or the entire cloud library, contact DrillOps at the address below. Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes the local library and saved café code. Remove exported files separately from their saved location.
